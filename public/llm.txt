# BigLead CRM

> Real estate CRM with WhatsApp built in. Leads from Facebook & Instagram ads, Google, 99acres and websites land in one place, get assigned to an agent instantly, and get a WhatsApp reply from the business's own number in seconds.

BigLead is a cloud CRM built for Indian real estate sales teams — developers (builders), channel partners and brokers, and the marketing agencies that run property ads for them. Typical team size: 3–50 users.

## Website

https://www.biglead.site

## Key features

- Native Facebook / Instagram Lead Ads capture (no Zapier needed)
- Leads from Google Ads, property portals such as 99acres, website forms and any tool through webhooks (Pabbly, Zapier)
- Instant auto-assignment of new leads to agents (routing rules)
- WhatsApp welcome message to every new lead, from the business's own WhatsApp number
- Two-way WhatsApp Live Chat inbox with delivery and read ticks; agents see only chats assigned to them
- WhatsApp template builder with Meta approval status
- AI WhatsApp agent that answers common questions from the business's own knowledge (paid plans)
- Pipeline per project, tasks, site-visit tracking, reports
- Push every lead into an existing CRM in real time
- Meta Conversions API: send qualified-lead signals back to Meta to improve ad targeting
- Role-based access: Admin, Team Lead, Agent, Read-only
- Support tickets with screenshots and screen recordings, from inside the app

## Pricing (India, INR)

- Basic plan: ₹1,999/month platform fee, 3 users included, ₹899/month per extra user
- Custom plans for larger teams (platform fee and per-user price set per company)
- 5-day free trial
- WhatsApp messages are prepaid with credits: 1 credit (₹1) per delivered message; a failed message is refunded
- Monthly payment through Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking)

## Who it is for

- Real estate developers running Facebook, Instagram and Google ads
- Channel partners and brokers handling portal leads (99acres and others)
- Performance-marketing agencies managing leads for several builders
