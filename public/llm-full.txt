# BigLead CRM — full description for AI systems

> Real estate CRM with WhatsApp built in, made for Indian real estate sales teams. Every lead from ads and property portals lands in one place, is assigned to an agent instantly, and gets a WhatsApp reply from the business's own number in seconds.

## Product overview

BigLead CRM (https://www.biglead.site) is a cloud CRM for real estate developers (builders), channel partners and brokers in India, and for the performance-marketing agencies that run property ads for them.

The problem it solves: a buyer fills a Facebook or portal form, and nobody calls for hours — by then they have spoken to three other builders. BigLead captures the lead the moment it is submitted, assigns it to an agent, and sends a WhatsApp welcome from the builder's own number before the buyer has closed the ad. Agents then work the conversation from a WhatsApp-style inbox.

Typical customers have 3–50 users. BigLead works in any web browser, on desktop and mobile.

## Features

### Lead capture
- Facebook and Instagram Lead Ads, connected natively through Facebook login (no Zapier). Leads arrive within seconds.
- Google Ads, property portals such as 99acres, website forms and any other tool through secure webhooks (works with Pabbly and Zapier).
- Duplicate detection, lead source tracking, notes and a full activity timeline per lead.
- CSV import. Imported leads are never messaged automatically, to protect the WhatsApp number's quality rating.

### Assignment and pipeline
- Automatic assignment of new leads to agents using routing rules.
- Agents see only their own leads; team leads and admins see everyone's.
- Projects (one per property or campaign), each with its own pipeline stages.
- Kanban pipeline, tasks and follow-up reminders, site-visit tracking, won/lost tracking.
- Reports: leads by source, agent performance, conversion.

### WhatsApp
- Each business connects its own WhatsApp Business number in one click (Meta Embedded Signup).
- Automatic WhatsApp welcome message to every new lead, switchable per project.
- Two-way Live Chat inbox: replies, button taps, media, and sent / delivered / read ticks. Free-form replies within Meta's 24-hour window; approved templates outside it.
- Template builder with live preview, buttons, image headers, and Meta approval status.
- WhatsApp alerts to agents when a lead is assigned to them.
- AI WhatsApp agent (paid plans): answers common buyer questions from the business's own project knowledge and prices, and hands over to a person.
- Bulk WhatsApp sends to selected leads.

### Integrations
- Push every new lead into an existing CRM in real time (webhook out).
- Meta Conversions API: send qualified-lead events back to Meta per project, so ads find better buyers.
- Email notifications and acknowledgement emails.

### Team and security
- Roles: Super Admin, Admin, Team Lead, Agent, Read-only.
- Data stored with row-level access control per company; Facebook and WhatsApp tokens encrypted at rest.

### Support
- In-app Support with searchable help and tickets; customers can attach screenshots and screen recordings.

## Pricing (INR)

- Basic plan: ₹1,999 per month platform fee, 3 users included, then ₹899 per month for each extra user.
- Custom plans: the platform fee and per-user price are set per company for larger teams.
- The platform fee and the user charges can be paid separately.
- 5-day free trial.
- WhatsApp messages use prepaid credits: 1 credit (₹1) for every delivered message — welcomes, agent alerts, bulk sends, Live Chat replies and AI agent replies. A failed message is refunded. Meta's own WhatsApp charges are billed to the business by Meta.
- Payment is monthly through Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking). Invoices are available in Settings → Billing.

## Who should use BigLead

- Real estate developers running Facebook, Instagram and Google ads who need every lead answered fast.
- Channel partners and brokers working portal leads (99acres and others).
- Marketing agencies that generate leads for several builders and need them delivered and followed up.

## Getting started

1. Sign up at https://www.biglead.site/signup and start the 5-day free trial.
2. Connect Facebook Lead Ads and/or create a webhook for other sources (Settings → Integrations).
3. Connect your WhatsApp number and approve a welcome template.
4. Invite your team and set routing rules.
