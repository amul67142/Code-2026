## What Trident Parktown is
Trident Parktown is Panipat's FIRST premium integrated township, envisaged over 125+ acres in Sector 19A & 40, Nizampur Road, Panipat, Haryana. It brings together residential plots, independent floors, group housing and commercial spaces across seven chakra-inspired neighbourhoods, with 10 theme-based parks and an ultra-modern clubhouse. Phase 1 (Trident Parktown 1) covers about 60 acres. Developed by Trident Park Town Private Limited, part of Trident Realty. HRERA registered.
